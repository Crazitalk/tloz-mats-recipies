How results are worked out

Sell price = sum of ingredient sell values × a factor for the number of items (1: 1.5, 2: 1.8, 3: 2.1, 4: 2.4, 5: 2.8), rounded down, then up to the next 10. Failed recipes sell for 2. Net gain = sell price minus the ingredients' raw sell value.

Hearts: cooking doubles each ingredient's raw hearts, capped at your hearts. A Hearty dish fully restores and adds the points as temporary hearts. Elixirs restore none. Fish, eggs, honey and a few others have no heart data and count as 0. Effects: points add up, one effect per pot (mixed effects cancel), and tiers use the wiki thresholds. Points come from the community ingredient chart; Energizing amounts are the stamina fifths each item restores. Duration: each ingredient adds its listed time; extra copies of pantry items (eggs, dairy, grains, spices) and dragon parts add 30 s; capped at 30:00. Energizing is capped by your stamina. "Balanced" duration counts up to 10:00 only, then prefers the cheaper pot.

Look Up only uses ingredients currently shown (filters and recipe search apply). To keep it fast, the finder only combines the best owned candidates (those carrying the chosen effect plus the top fillers by price, hearts and duration). A pot only counts as a recipe if every item belongs to it. The game's possible buy-price cap isn't modelled.
